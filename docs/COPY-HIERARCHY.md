## Supporting copy: how it works

Headlines identify the offer and audience. Section introductions and card titles explain the decisions: find waste, build the investment case, implement, verify; choose REEF delivery support or implementation by the client's team.

## Detail: evidence and conditions

Body copy supplies scope, documented Allianz results, measurement assumptions and conditional external-financing terms. These details support the proposition without carrying the entire explanation. No invented metric is used as a decorative graph.
