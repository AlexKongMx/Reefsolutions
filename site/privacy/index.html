Website enquiries

How we handle your information.

This notice covers enquiries submitted to Resource Efficiency Consulting Sdn. Bhd., trading as REEF Solutions, through this website.

What you share

The assessment form collects your name, company, work email, role, building type, location, approximate energy spend and description of your requirements. Please do not include confidential building records, financial account details or other sensitive information in the initial enquiry.

Why we use it

We use these details to respond, assess whether our services fit your requirements and discuss a possible assessment. Submitting an enquiry does not sign you up for a marketing list.

How enquiries are processed

The website and form are hosted by Netlify. Form submissions are processed and stored through Netlify’s services and may be processed outside Malaysia. REEF uses the submitted details to manage your enquiry.

Your questions and requests

To ask about the information you have provided, request a correction or request deletion, contact reefsolutions.co@gmail.com.

Links to other websites

External links, including social profiles, are subject to the privacy practices of those services. This website does not embed advertising trackers or third-party analytics.
