Commercial offices / Kuala Lumpur

Allianz Malaysia: 16,400 kWh less every month.

Targeted improvements across 13 office floors at Menara Allianz Sentral and Plaza Sentral.

The opportunity

Energy consumption was spread across lighting, air conditioning and equipment used in the everyday running of a corporate headquarters. REEF reviewed how those systems were operated, then identified improvements across two buildings.

What changed

  • Lighting: improvements to lighting systems and how they were operated.
  • HVAC: optimisation of air-conditioning operation.
  • Building retrofits: targeted upgrades identified through the assessment.
  • Operating routines: reducing unnecessary running time for printers, water dispensers and air-conditioning units.

The reported results

REEF’s company profiles report an average reduction of 16,400 kWh per month, with average monthly financial savings of approximately RM 8,900. Project follow-up notes state that improvements appeared on the electricity bill the month after implementation.

The engagement used a shared-savings arrangement. That historical structure does not imply that other projects qualify for the same commercial terms.

What the numbers mean for your building

These are historical results for this engagement. A new property needs its own assessment, baseline and investment case. Savings depend on equipment, tariffs, occupancy and operating conditions.

The client’s baseline consumption is confidential. We do not publish an energy-reduction percentage or extrapolate this result to other buildings.
