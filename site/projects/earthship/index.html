Earthship Malaysia.

Bringing independent energy and water systems into community buildings.

Designed as one system

The documented Earthship Malaysia project comprises two houses and a community centre, combining building design with resource systems.

Integrated scope

  • Solar energy generation.
  • Rainwater harvesting and greywater recycling.
  • On-site sewage treatment.
  • Passive temperature control and food planters.

Experience that informs our work

The project demonstrates experience in coordinating energy, water and building operation. We apply that same whole-system perspective to commercial property assessments, with solutions tailored to each site.
