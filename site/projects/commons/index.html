Solar + water / Iskandar, Johor

The Commons, EduCity.

Renewable generation and water systems designed around an educational space.

Energy and water, considered together

The Commons brought solar generation and rainwater harvesting into the operation of two buildings at EduCity, Iskandar.

Scope of work

  • A bifacial solar tree and supplementary rooftop panels to power lighting, fans and other electrical loads.
  • Rainwater collection and storage.
  • Filtration to support the site’s water needs.

Practical systems in a shared space

The project illustrates REEF’s experience integrating resource systems around real building uses. The company profile documents the installed scope. Project-specific cost savings and payback figures are not published here.
