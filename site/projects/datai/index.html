A whole-property view

A resort’s resource needs extend across buildings, infrastructure, guest services and everyday operations. REEF’s documented work at The Datai Langkawi addresses those systems through sustainability assessment and management planning.

  • Waste reduction and resource recovery.
  • Operational practices and community engagement.

From assessment to priorities

The work creates a framework for identifying improvements and embedding better practices in day-to-day operations.

This project is presented for its assessment and management scope. No measured reduction in the resort’s energy or water consumption is claimed here.
